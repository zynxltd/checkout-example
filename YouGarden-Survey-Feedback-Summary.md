# YouGarden Website Survey — Stakeholder Summary

**Survey question:** What is the main thing we could do to improve your experience on the YouGarden website?  
**Period:** Aug 2026 (Crazy Egg)  
**Responses:** 38 total · ~70% left a written answer  
**Overall sentiment:** Mostly negative (≈53% negative)

---

## Executive summary

Customers are broadly able to browse the site, but a meaningful share struggle to **complete an order**, then feel unsure whether it worked, and some receive plants in poorer condition than expected. These issues are costing conversions and damaging trust.

The single highest-priority fix is the **basket → payment → confirmation** journey.

---

## Key findings

### 1. Checkout and basket failures *(highest priority)*
Multiple customers report being unable to buy:
- Items not adding to the basket (including during discount offers)
- Basket not viewable / page scroll stuck
- Unable to reach payment or payment failing without a clear reason
- Having to place the order several times and still not knowing if it succeeded

**Business impact:** Direct lost orders; at least one customer said they would shop elsewhere.

### 2. Order confirmation and delivery communication
After purchase (or attempted purchase), customers lack confidence:
- No clear confirmation or visibility of outstanding orders
- Delayed or missing deliveries with little or no proactive update
- Difficulty contacting or getting help from customer service

### 3. Plant quality vs expectation on arrival
Recurring complaints about condition on delivery:
- Plants arriving dry, shedding leaves, or “dead / half dead on arrival”
- Requests for realistic “out of the box” product photos
- Requests for clearer potting / planting care instructions with the plants

### 4. Membership and offers not meeting expectations
- Join-club / 10% discount not applied
- Long-standing members unclear why they receive no discount
- Request for member pre-order when items are out of stock

### 5. Website usability friction
- Site described as hard to use; upsells interrupting before checkout
- Difficulty editing basket quantities
- Login, password reset, and email recognition issues
- App verification problems

---

## What’s working

Some customers report positive experiences: competitive prices, healthy plants, and delivery arriving earlier than expected. When fulfilment works well, satisfaction is high.

---

## Recommended priorities for stakeholders

| Priority | Focus | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Fix add-to-basket, checkout, and payment reliability | Clear conversion leakage |
| **P1** | Clear order confirmation + order history visibility | Reduces repeat attempts and support load |
| **P2** | Proactive delay / delivery updates | Rebuilds post-purchase trust |
| **P2** | Arrival quality + realistic imagery / care guides | Aligns expectation with product received |
| **P3** | Membership discounts applied reliably + clearer member benefits | Protects loyalty programme value |
| **P3** | Reduce checkout interruption (upsells) and simplify basket edits | Improves ease of purchase |

---

## Suggested next steps

1. Investigate and fix basket/checkout/payment defects (desktop + tablet), with particular attention to failed add-to-basket and failed payment with no error message.
2. Audit order confirmation emails, account order history, and “order success” messaging.
3. Review packing/dispatch QA and consider “as shipped” imagery plus care cards.
4. Audit club discount application and membership messaging.
5. Re-run a short pulse survey after fixes to measure sentiment and checkout success.
