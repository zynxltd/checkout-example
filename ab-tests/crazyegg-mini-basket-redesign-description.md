# Mini basket redesign (desktop)

**Hypothesis:** A clearer, modern mini-basket will make basket contents easier to scan and encourage more users to open the full basket / continue toward checkout.

**Audience:** Desktop only — viewports **1000px and wider**.

**Metric ideas:** Mini-basket opens, “View Basket” clicks, product title/image clicks in the dropdown, basket page views, add-to-basket → basket rate.

---

## Control

Current header mini-basket dropdown: dark brown/taupe panel, white text, gold product numbers, square thumbnails, green “View Basket »” button. Images and titles are not linked.

**URL:** https://www.yougarden.com/

## Variation

Restyled mini-basket to match the light mockup:

- White panel, rounded corners, soft shadow
- Beige “Your Basket” header with item count
- Circular product thumbnails
- Dark title + muted “Product No.” / “Qty” copy
- Pink pill “View Basket” CTA
- Product **images** and **titles** link through to the PDP (`/item-p-{number}/{slug}`)
